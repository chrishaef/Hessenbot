# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Hessenbot and the Meshhessen community spaces around it to be a respectful place for operators, developers, and radio amateurs.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Assume good intent; disagree on ideas, not people
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Do not share others’ private data, credentials, or location details without consent
- Respect local amateur-radio and network rules

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples include harassment, trolling, publishing private information (“doxxing”), or deliberately sabotaging mesh or project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive reports, or open a private channel via the maintainer on GitHub). Maintainers may warn, moderate, or block contributors who violate this code.
